# andydrice.com

Andy Rice's personal home on the web, publishing finished essays at permanent addresses and introducing Andy to a first-time visitor. This glossary names the things the site is made of so that issues, commits, and design notes use one word for each.

## Language

### Writing

**Essay**:
A finished piece of writing with a permanent address, an honest date, and frozen text. The unit of the site.
_Avoid_: post, article, blog post, note

**Vault draft**:
The working copy of an essay in Andy's Obsidian vault. It may keep changing after publication.
_Avoid_: source, original

**Text of record**:
The copy of an essay on the site, frozen at publication. Where the site and the vault draft differ, the site is right.
_Avoid_: final draft, published version

**Ship date**:
The date an essay went live. It is the essay's only date and is never backdated to when the draft was written.
_Avoid_: publish date, written date, created date

**Slug**:
The hand-chosen last segment of an essay's address. Never derived from a filename or a title.
_Avoid_: permalink, path, URL name

**Description**:
The one approved sentence that says what an essay is about. It appears under the title and in link previews.
_Avoid_: excerpt, summary, subtitle, teaser

**Bio line**:
The first two sentences of the home paragraph, used anywhere a one-line introduction of Andy is needed.
_Avoid_: tagline, byline, about blurb

**Canonical home**:
The essay's address on this site. Any copy elsewhere points back to it.
_Avoid_: original link, source URL

**Syndication copy**:
A copy of an essay published on another service that points back to the canonical home.
_Avoid_: cross-post, repost, mirror

### Pages

**Home page**:
The one-paragraph page at the root that introduces Andy and opens onto the essays list. It never lists essays.
_Avoid_: index, landing page, front page

**Essays list**:
The page listing every essay, newest first.
_Avoid_: archive, blog, feed, index

**Entry**:
One essay's place on the essays list: its rule, ship date, title, and description, all one link.
_Avoid_: item, card, row

**Newest**:
The most recent essay on the list. It alone is set larger.
_Avoid_: featured, latest post, hero

**Visited entry**:
An entry whose essay the reader has already opened in this browser. Its rule has faded to grey.
_Avoid_: read, seen

### Parts of a page

**Rail**:
The wayfinding column on the left of every page. It holds the two site links, and on an essay the dateline and the mark.
_Avoid_: sidebar, header, nav bar, menu

**Dateline**:
The "Published" line and ship date in the rail of an essay.
_Avoid_: byline, meta, timestamp

**Rule**:
A heavy horizontal bar. The ink rule opens the reading column on the home page and on an essay; a spot rule heads every entry.
_Avoid_: divider, border, line, separator

**Reading column**:
The single column of words to the right of the rail. Its width is set by the text's role, not the page.
_Avoid_: content area, main, container

**Drop initial**:
The first letter of the home paragraph or of an essay, set large in the spot and dropped into the first lines.
_Avoid_: drop cap, initial cap, lettrine

**Standfirst**:
The essay's description as it appears in italic under the title.
_Avoid_: subtitle, deck, lede, subheading

**Endmark**:
The small spot square that closes the last paragraph of an essay.
_Avoid_: end sign, tombstone, dingbat

**Mark**:
The plumb bob silhouette, in one colour, placed once per page and somewhere different on each. It is never joined to the name.
_Avoid_: logo, icon, brand mark, favicon, lockup

### Colour

**Paper**:
The page background, warm in both schemes. The only background the site has.
_Avoid_: canvas, surface, page colour

**Ink**:
The colour of the words, the rail, the opening rule, and the mark.
_Avoid_: text colour, foreground, black

**Spot**:
The one accent colour on a page: the drop initial, the entry rules, the endmark, the current-page dot, and link ink. Chosen per essay; the house spot is terracotta.
_Avoid_: accent, brand colour, primary, highlight, theme colour

**House spot**:
The terracotta that applies to any page that has not chosen its own spot.
_Avoid_: default colour, fallback

**Scheme**:
Light or dark, following the reader's system setting. Every colour has a rendition in each.
_Avoid_: theme, mode, skin
